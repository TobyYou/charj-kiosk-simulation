# joint_calibration_c_wtp_2026-11-13.csv
- **What:** simulator output (not external data). History-matching grid for the conversion factor c (renters per passer-by at evening peak) × wtp_mid_hourly (logistic slope 2), 13-station network, normal week, 20 reps per point, the same seeds at $1.00, $1.50 and $2.00/hr (common random numbers). Produced by `node research/scripts/joint_calibration_c_wtp.mjs 20 20261113 2026-11-13` (~70 s) BEFORE wtp_mid was changed (all other params at 2026-11-12 defaults).
- **Columns:** c, wtp_mid, rps_1 (rentals/station/day at $1/hr) ± se, rps_2 (at $2/hr), eps_arc_1to2 (paired arc elasticity of rentals between $1 and $2/hr) ± se, profit/week at $1 (± se), $1.50, $2; best_price (of the three); implausibilities I_rentals, I_elasticity, I_max; non_implausible (I_max ≤ 3).
- **Implausibility (STATS_TOOLKIT M10):** I = |z − E f| / sqrt(V_obs + V_disc + V_MC). Rentals on log scale: z = ln(√(0.5·3)), V_obs = (ln 6 / 4)² (band edges = ±2σ), V_disc = 0.2². Elasticity: z = −0.6, V_obs = 0.2², V_disc = 0.1².
- **Headline:** the elasticity target pins wtp_mid to ≈1.9–2.6 at every c (I_E ≤ 1.1); c is NOT pinned by the wide 0.5–3 band (all c in 6e-5…3.6e-4 are non-implausible). With the price-matched ChargeSPOT Australia band (0.48–0.84) as the target instead, c ≈ 6e-5–1e-4 (I ≤ 3 only there). At wtp 2.0–2.5, $2/hr beats $1/hr on profit at every c.
- **Caveats:** elasticity target is a transfer from Japan/Taiwan power banks and US bikeshare (P075, P088, P091); the logistic slope 2 is fixed; no repeat-use/habit dynamics (Weschke 2024; INFORICH youth churn) are modelled.
