# mnpd_calls_hourly_broadway_vandy_2025.csv
- **Source:** Metro Nashville Police Department, Calls for Service 2025 (Nashville GIS ArcGIS FeatureServer `MNPD_Calls_for_Service_2025_view/FeatureServer/0`), https://services2.arcgis.com/HdTo6HJqh92wn4D8/arcgis/rest/services/MNPD_Calls_for_Service_2025_view/FeatureServer/0/query . 2024 replication: `Metro_Nashville_Police_Department_Calls_for_Service_2024/FeatureServer/0`.
- **Downloaded:** 2026-11-13 (research agent); re-aggregated by the orchestrator from the same JSON, output identical.
- **Zones (envelope, WGS84):** broadway = -86.7805,36.1585,-86.7730,36.1645 (Lower Broadway 1st–5th Ave + side blocks; 5,088 calls); vandy = -86.808,36.1395,-86.796,36.153 (Vanderbilt / West End / 21st Ave; 2,267 calls). Coordinates rounded to 0.001° (~100 m).
- **Time:** `Call_Received` is UTC; converted to America/Chicago. Check: MNPD shift codes align (A 06–14, B 15–22, C 23–05 local). Day type by *service day* starting 05:00 local (1 a.m. Saturday counts as Friday night). 2025: 209 Mon–Thu, 104 Fri/Sat, 52 Sun days.
- **Columns:** zone, daytype, local_hour_CT, calls_2025, calls_per_day, share_of_daytype.
- **Use / caveats:** police calls are a proxy for nightlife crowd *intensity shape*, not headcount; they over-weight late drunk/disorderly hours. 15:00 spike is a shift-change artefact. The vandy zone shows NO term effect (term-month weekday 07–22 h 5.62 calls/day vs Jun–Jul 5.84) — it is dominated by VUMC; do not use it as a campus footfall proxy.
- **Scripts:** `research/scripts/mnpd_fetch_calls.sh` (fetch; args NAME xmin ymin xmax ymax OUTDIR), `mnpd_calls_hourly.py` (aggregate), `mnpd_compare_years.py` (2024 vs 2025), `broadway_night_profile.py` (smoothed shape). Raw JSON kept in the session scratchpad only.
