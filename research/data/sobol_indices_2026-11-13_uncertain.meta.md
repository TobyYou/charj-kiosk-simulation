# sobol_indices_2026-11-13_uncertain.csv

- **What:** simulator output (not external data). Variance-based (Sobol) sensitivity of weekly profit ($/week) and rentals per station per day for the 13-station Charj network, normal week, nightly rebalancing on.
- **Produced by:** `node research/scripts/sobol_indices.mjs 512 6 20261113` on 2026-11-13_uncertain; 5120 model evaluations × 6 reps = 30720 simulation runs, 793 s.
- **Design:** Saltelli (2010) A/B/AB_i scheme with iid uniform samples (mulberry32(20261113)); first-order S1 by Saltelli 2010 estimator, total ST by Jansen (1999). 95% CIs: 500 bootstrap resamples of the N rows. Each evaluation is the mean of 6 replications with a fixed seed set (CRN), so residual Monte Carlo noise is partly absorbed into the inputs' effect; with CRN it is small relative to the factor variance.
- **Factors (k=8):** top-8 by Morris μ* on 2026-11-11, uniform over params.js [low, high]: c [0.000059, 0.00039]; rebalance_cost [4, 15]; forfeit_churn_cost [0, 25]; p_stay [0.25, 0.75]; away_return_median_h [6, 20]; forfeit_dispute_rate [0.05, 0.35]; wtp_mid_hourly [2, 5]; p_forget [0.01, 0.1]. c varied via p_aware = c/(p_low_peak×p_seek). packs_per_kiosk uniform over integers 4..8. All other parameters fixed at defaults, so indices are shares of the variance *generated by these 8 factors*, not of all uncertainty.
- **Output summary:** profit mean $218.5/wk, SD $157.8; rentals mean 1.876, SD 0.738. ΣS1 (profit) = 0.990.
- **Caveats:** uniform ranges are mostly ASSUMPTION widths, and indices scale with them. Estimator negatives near 0 are sampling noise. Normal-week scenario only.
