# sobol_indices_2026-11-12.csv

- **What:** simulator output (not external data). Variance-based (Sobol) sensitivity of weekly profit ($/week) and rentals per station per day for the 13-station Charj network, normal week, nightly rebalancing on.
- **Produced by:** `node research/scripts/sobol_indices.mjs 512 6 20261112` on 2026-11-12; 5120 model evaluations × 6 reps = 30720 simulation runs, 616 s.
- **Design:** Saltelli (2010) A/B/AB_i scheme with iid uniform samples (mulberry32(20261112)); first-order S1 by Saltelli 2010 estimator, total ST by Jansen (1999). 95% CIs: 500 bootstrap resamples of the N rows. Each evaluation is the mean of 6 replications with a fixed seed set (CRN), so residual Monte Carlo noise is partly absorbed into the inputs' effect; with CRN it is small relative to the factor variance.
- **Factors (k=8):** top-8 by Morris μ* on 2026-11-11, uniform over params.js [low, high]: c [0.000059, 0.00039]; packs_per_kiosk [4, 8]; host_share [0, 0.5]; rebalance_cost [6, 25]; forfeit_churn_cost [0, 25]; away_return_median_h [6, 20]; forfeit_dispute_rate [0.05, 0.35]; p_stay [0.25, 0.75]. c varied via p_aware = c/(p_low_peak×p_seek). packs_per_kiosk uniform over integers 4..8. All other parameters fixed at defaults, so indices are shares of the variance *generated by these 8 factors*, not of all uncertainty.
- **Output summary:** profit mean $23.0/wk, SD $214.5; rentals mean 1.874, SD 0.733. ΣS1 (profit) = 0.745.
- **Caveats:** uniform ranges are mostly ASSUMPTION widths, and indices scale with them. Estimator negatives near 0 are sampling noise. Normal-week scenario only.
- **Parameter vintage (important):** the run started before today's params.js/geo.js edits were saved, so it used rebalance_cost range **6–25** (old) and MTA-2024 weekendFactors. Under the new range 4–15 the variance generated by rebalance_cost scales by ≈(11/19)² ≈ 0.34, so its share would roughly fall from 0.23 to ≈0.09 (rough, ignoring renormalisation). Re-run with `node research/scripts/sobol_indices.mjs 512 6 20261112 <date>` for current ranges.
- **Interpretation:** packs_per_kiosk (ST 0.32) and host_share (0.19) are *decisions / negotiable terms*, not uncertainties; they should be optimised or set by scenario, not lumped into the uncertainty budget. Among genuine unknowns, c (ST 0.22, S1 0.10 → most of its effect is via interactions, chiefly with stocking) and rebalance_cost dominate; forfeit_churn_cost, p_stay, away_return_median_h, forfeit_dispute_rate each ≤0.06.
