# Charj sim research — 2026-11-11 (scheduled run)

## Summary
- **The hourly share of phones below 20% is now measured, not assumed.** We computed it from the open ExtraSensory dataset (UCSD, 60 users, 377k battery readings), counting only time when the phone is unplugged, since someone walking past a kiosk isn't on a charger.
  - Weekdays peak at 21:00 at **0.148** (90% CI 0.10–0.19), which matches our p_low_peak of 0.12–0.135.
  - Weekend late nights reach **0.30**.
  - The hand-drawn LOW_BATTERY_CURVE is replaced by data-derived weekday and weekend curves. The biggest change: the 17–19 h commute peak is lower than we had assumed.
- **Both engine artefacts that Morris flagged are fixed.**
  - Rebalancing now never leaves a station full.
  - Forfeits now carry dispute and churn costs.
  - More packs and more forgetting no longer raise profit: 7–8 packs/station fall to $136–182/wk, against $388 at 5–6.
  - Two assumption-only costs now sit in the Morris top 5: rebalancing cost (μ* $227/wk) and forfeit churn (μ* $171/wk).
- **The best US-like benchmark points to lower demand than our default.** ChargeSPOT Australia, at about US$7–8 per rental, runs **0.48–0.84 rentals/station/day**. Japan runs 1.27 and Taiwan 1.4. Our default sits at 2.92, the top of the band. Australia-level conversion would mean roughly $0–100/wk of profit instead of $416. Deciding where to centre c is now the most important open question.

## Methods learned (→ STATS_TOOLKIT)
- **M9 (new):** a low-battery share conditional on the phone being unplugged.
  - Equal-user and time-weighted, with a cluster-bootstrap CI.
  - Weekend curve shrunk toward the weekday curve.
  - Validated by an exact re-run and a time-zone flag check (377,027/377,027 rows agree).
- **M8 update:** the 2-component mixture fit is weakly identified (1 residual degree of freedom).
  - The June and September fits disagree on the "away" component: median 25 h vs 7.6 h.
  - They agree on a "stay" component holding 73–92% of rentals, with a median of 86–107 min.
  - So calibrate the stay share and stay median, not away_return_median_h, from operator bins.

## Biggest remaining uncertainties (by impact on profit)
1. **Conversion level c:** $0–100/wk at Australia-like rates, $163/wk at 1.5/day, $416/wk at the default. This is now backed by a price-matched comparable and needs a decision (Toby) or field data.
2. **Operating cost assumptions:** rebalance cost per stop ($6–25) and churn per forfeit ($0–25) are each worth about $170–230/wk across their range. Both can be pinned down cheaply with an ops plan.
3. **Hold-time mix:** the engine's stay holds are much shorter than Japan's. The effect of per-started-hour pricing on holds is unknown.
