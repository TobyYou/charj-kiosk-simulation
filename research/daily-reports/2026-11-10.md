# Charj sim research — 2026-11-10 (scheduled run)

## Summary
- **Morris global sensitivity analysis now runs** (plain JS, 35 factors, ~150 s). It also caught an engine bug: full stations *raised* profit, because failed returns kept billing and then forfeited the $30 deposit. Fixed with Charj's own grace policy: the clock pauses at the first failed return (GAME_PLAN §1.3). After the fix, the profit levers rank: c ($379/wk across its range) > venue share ($255) > away-hold time ($237) > p_stay ($199) > packs per station ($176). Phone and pack physics are below the noise floor.
- **First real rental-duration histogram found** (INFORICH/ChargeSPOT Japan, June 2024): 10% of rentals under 30 min, 26% under 1 h, 64% under 3 h, 5.1% at 24 h or more; median ≈133 min. Hong Kong 2024 agrees (30% under 1 h, 76% under 3 h). This contradicts the old calibration target ">60% within 1 h" (an INFORICH press line about *users*), which has been downgraded. The simulator's 70-min median now sits between Brick (45 min, €1/h like Charj) and Asia. p_stay alone swings profit **$336–652/week**, making it the second-biggest open uncertainty after c.
- **Per-station subway profiles for all five 1-train stations** (MTA OD 2025, weekday + weekend). 103 St is the most residential (AM entries ≈2× exits) and 116 St is a destination (AM exits 3.2× entries). Network profit barely moved ($496 → $493/wk). New Nashville anchors: WeGo Route 3 West End about 2,000 riders/day (Q2 2026), but Vanderbilt commuters are only 5% transit and 13% walk, so transit can't anchor Nashville footfall alone.

## Methods learned (→ STATS_TOOLKIT)
- **M4 updated (run):**
  - Morris with p = 4, r = 20, k = 35, 10 CRN reps per point.
  - The noise floor is about $16/wk. CRN cuts elementary-effect noise about 2.7×.
  - Run it after every engine change as a bug detector.
- **M8 (new):** an interval-censored MLE for operator charts that publish shares per bin.
  - The single lognormal underfits the ≥24 h tail.
  - A stay/away mixture is the right shape, which is what the engine already has.

## Biggest remaining uncertainties (by impact on profit)
1. **Conversion level c:** $230/wk at 1.8e-4 vs $471/wk at 3.6e-4. Needs the street survey or pilot data, or Toby's decision.
2. **Hold-time mix (p_stay, away_return_median_h):** $336–652/wk. ChargeSPOT bins now give a target, but Japan's flat 30 min–3 h tariff likely lengthens holds compared with Charj's per-started-hour billing.
3. **Profit definition artefacts:**
  - Rebalancing never fires at 7–8 packs.
  - Forfeits and overdue fees count as pure gain.
  - These distort the packs-per-station and forgetfulness levers until fixed.
