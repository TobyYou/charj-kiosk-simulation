## Methods learned (→ STATS_TOOLKIT)
- **M10, history matching:**
  - Implausibility I = |z − Ef| / √(V_obs + V_disc + V_MC), with a 3σ cut-off (Pukelsheim).
  - Using a paired CRN elasticity as a target makes MC noise negligible.
  - Elasticity identifies wtp_mid; the rentals band identifies c, but only weakly.
- **M11, EVPI/EVPPI** (Strong–Oakley regression estimator), to rank research by decision value rather than variance (Felli & Hazen). Planned, not yet run.
- **M4 update:** decompose uncertain inputs only, and sweep decisions first. A Sobol "importance" can be a cliff inside a decision's range.
- **M8 note:** binned-share fits need an assumed effective N for CIs. Use a G-test with df = K − 1 − q, and compare against simulated bins at the data's N.
