# Charj sim research — 2026-11-09 (scheduled run)

## Summary
- **The calibration only identifies one number.** The engine gives identical outputs whether the demand funnel is scaled through p_aware or through p_low_peak (CRN runs). So the 0.5–3 rentals/station/day benchmark pins only c = p_aware × p_low_peak × p_seek. The band corresponds to c ≈ 5.9e-5 to 3.9e-4.
  - The default c = 3.6e-4 sits near the top of the band, at 2.82/day and $492/week.
  - The prior centre, c = 2.0e-4, gives about 1.6/day and about $240/week.
  - Where to centre c is now a decision for Toby, not a research gap (STATS_TOOLKIT M7, P055).
- **MTA origin–destination data shows exits ≈ entries at 116 St only over a full day.** In the 17:00–20:00 window, exits are just 0.49× entries; at 7–10 am they are about 3.2×.
  - The weekday subway footfall profile is now entries + exits from MTA OD 2025.
  - This moves 6.5 percentage points of daily footfall from the evening to the morning.
  - Result: rentals 2.93 → 2.82/station/day and profit $531 → $492/week. Still inside the band.
- **There is still no hourly "share of phones below 20%" anywhere.**
  - Two papers now bound it from below: Li 2018 (41% of discharge sessions end below 20%, 5% die) and Guo 2017 (80,000 phones; charging-start levels roughly uniform).
  - The p_low_peak range low end was raised from 0.04 to 0.05. The value stays at 0.12.
  - Citi Bike (Oct 2026, 49k Morningside trips) is a poor proxy for how long people hold a pack: its median trip is 9 min, while ChargeSPOT reports only "over 60%" returned within 1 h.

## Biggest remaining uncertainties (by impact on profit)
1. **Level of c** (equivalently p_aware for a given p_low and p_seek): profit is ~$240 at c = 2.0e-4 and ~$492 at 3.6e-4 (the default), within one benchmark band. Needs pilot data or a decision.
2. **How long people keep a pack (duration tail):** this drives revenue per rental at $1/hr and forfeits. There is no operator distribution, and Citi Bike is too short to stand in for it.
3. **Hourly shape of low-battery share and footfall at the non-116 St stations:** this decides where kiosks go. The OD dataset can fix the footfall half.
