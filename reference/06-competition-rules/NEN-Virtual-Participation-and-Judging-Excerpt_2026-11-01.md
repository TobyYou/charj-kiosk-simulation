**JUDGING AND GRANT AWARDS**

Initial Judging. During the week of November 27, 2026 – December 4, 2026 a panel of judges selected by Sponsor (“Judging Panel”) will evaluate all eligible Submissions based solely on the following equally weighted criteria (“Criteria”):

I. Originality of Idea (10 points)

Will be evaluated for the degree to which the business idea is original, creative, and distinct from existing products, services, or solutions.

II. Problem/Opportunity (10 points)

Will be evaluated for the degree to which the business idea clearly identifies and addresses a meaningful problem or opportunity, including why now is the right time to pursue it.

III. Passion and Conviction (10 points)

Will be evaluated for the degree to which the Participant demonstrates genuine personal commitment to, and belief in, the business idea.

IV. Ability to Execute (10 points)

Will be evaluated for the degree to which the Participant presents a credible, practical plan to advance the business idea, including how the Participant used the Mini-Grant and how the Participant would use $25,000.

V. Potential Impact (10 points)

Will be evaluated for the degree to which the business idea, if executed, could create meaningful value for customers, a community, or a market.
