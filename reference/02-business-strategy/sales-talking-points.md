# Sales talking points: numbers for venue conversations

October 6, 2026. For in-person or call conversations with venue owners. **Not for the website.** These numbers need context, caveats and the owner's own inputs, which a conversation can give and a webpage can't.

## 1. Is the calculator example realistic?

The website's calculator starts at **15 paid rentals a day → about $180/month** for the venue (at $2/rental and a 20% share, 30 days), with presets from 5 to 25 a day.

- **15 rentals a day is a little more than one an hour** across a 12-hour day; 5 a day is about one every 2½ hours.
- **Benchmark:** Energy Monster, China's largest power-bank rental network, reported **CNY 2.81 billion revenue across ~664,000 venue locations in 2020** ([EqualOcean](https://equalocean.com/analysis/2021062816406), [Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2021-03-16/chinese-power-bank-rental-startup-energy-monster-files-for-us-ipo-101676126.html)).
  - That's about **CNY 4,200 per location per year (~$50/month gross)**, in a COVID year.
  - At typical Chinese prices of a few yuan per rental, that's roughly **4 rentals per location per day**. *This is our estimate: the filing reports revenue, not rentals.*

**How to say it:** "The largest network in the world averages roughly four rentals a day per venue. Our 15-a-day starting example is a busy venue, about four times that average, so let's also look at 5 a day, which is closer to the benchmark." Lead with the lower presets for quieter venues.

**Caveats to be upfront about:**
- China isn't New York. Rentals there cost a fraction of $2, and the habit is everywhere. Higher prices may mean fewer rentals; busier venues and phone-heavy guests may mean more.
- It's a benchmark, not a forecast.
- Ignore vendor blogs claiming 12–25 rentals a day per station. They're sales material from equipment sellers, not data.

## 2. What is the problem costing them today?

Work this out **with the owner, using their numbers**. Don't bring a pre-filled total.

| Cost | Formula | Example (illustration only) |
|---|---|---|
| **They leave** (lost sales) | charging requests per day × share who leave or skip another order × average order × days | 8 × 25% × $6 × 30 = **$360/mo** |
| **Staff pulled away** | requests per day × minutes spent each × hourly wage × days | 8 × 3 min × $18/hr × 30 = **$216/mo** |
| **Table stuck** (only for venues with a wait at peak times) | outlet-campers per day × extra minutes held ÷ normal table time × average table spend × days | 2 × 30 min ÷ 60 min × $25 × 30 = **$375/mo** |

- **The key question:** "How many guests ask to charge their phone on a typical day?" If they don't know, ask them to count for a week.
- **The pitch:** "Charj takes the staff time and outlet-camping off your plate, and you earn on top." Don't promise that every guest who would have left will now stay.
- **Turnover:** many cafés want tables to turn. Charj chargers are portable, so guests don't need the outlet seat. Never promise longer stays.

## 3. The most convincing number: our own

Run a **2–4 week pilot at 2–3 friendly venues** and record:
- Charging requests per day (staff tally)
- Paid rentals per day, and average rental length
- Return rate, and any issues
- The owner's opinion after the pilot

One real line, such as *"At [Café X] in Manhattan: N rentals a day in the first month"*, beats every benchmark above. Get the owner's permission before quoting them or using their name, and record the dates and context. Once there's real pilot data, it can go on the website.

## 4. Sources behind claims on the website

- **"15% have walked out of a café, bar or restaurant because they couldn't charge"** (section 2): a Chargifi/OnePoll survey of 2,000 UK consumers (2016–17), commissioned by a charging company. If an owner asks, say so plainly. It's dated and UK-based; replace it with your own data when you have it.
- **"Already everyday in China and Europe"** (hero): Energy Monster had 219M+ registered users across ~664,000 venue locations in China at the end of 2020 ([EqualOcean](https://equalocean.com/analysis/2021062816406)); ChargedUp runs 2,000+ stations across Europe ([UKSPA](https://www.ukspa.org.uk/chargedup-raises-2m-to-expand-mobile-charging-network/)). "Market-proven" refers to this business model, not to Charj's own track record.
