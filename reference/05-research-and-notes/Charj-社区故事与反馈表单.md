# Charj随行故事与使用反馈：表单优化稿

2026-10-30，America/Chicago。依据用户两张表单截图与本聊天要求；这是本地可复制方案，未编辑Google Form或发布。截图中的账户邮箱与原始图片不存档。用户本次品牌写法为Charj，沿用本次写法，不静默更改旧Charge文档。

## 用户目的与现状

- 参与者大部分为内部朋友，会把充电宝带到各地，含咖啡店、书店、学校等场景；实际地点/人数/时间未提供。
- 希望兼顾营销、市场调研及社媒素材；明确Charj是community，不仅是business。此为品牌意图，不把它记成已经形成活跃社群。
- 截图可见：标题Everywhere that Charj has been；两段长介绍；照片上传必填，最多5份、每份100MB；地点/出发地/目的地/故事合在一个长问句；另一个宽泛反馈题。截图未显示明确的社媒使用选择，不推断截图外不存在其他设置。

## 首版结构与停止点

保留一个主要二维码。先用四道简短使用问题，再进入完全自愿的故事分享。照片不做使用反馈的前置条件。基础反馈只需最近一次经历；未使用也能提交，不强迫给评价。实际填写时长先用朋友试填确认，再对外标时长。

不增加完整价格/押金/品牌认知量表；这些以后按具体决策单独验证。站点需求以实际低电量经历和可替代办法判断，不拿旅行照片分布当作付费市场地图。

## 可直接复制的英文表单

Title: **Where did Charj take you?**
Subtitle: **A little power. A story worth sharing.**

Intro:
Wherever your day takes you, we’d love to hear about it. Tell us about your latest experience with Charj, then share a photo or a small moment if you’d like.

Honest feedback—including what didn’t work—helps us build this together. Photos, stories, and joining future updates are optional. We’ll only share your contributions publicly with your permission.

Do not promise anonymity: file uploads and optional contact fields may identify respondents. State the actual owner/contact before launch; do not invent an email/handle.

### Short feedback

**1. Have you used this Charj power bank yet?**
Required / multiple choice:
- Yes, to charge a device
- I’ve carried it but haven’t used it yet
- Not yet

Routing: Yes → Q2–4; other options → optional Q2 (where carrying/expect to use, label accordingly), skip experience rating and improvements based on use; then optional stories. Do not force those who never used it to fabricate experience.

**2. Where did you last use it?**
Required if used / single choice:
- Campus / school
- Café / restaurant
- Library / bookstore
- Traveling / commuting
- Event / social gathering
- Home / accommodation
- Other: ___

Ask about the most recent use only, so categories describe one event. A venue name/city is optional later. Never require real-time exact location.

**3. How did it work for you that time?**
Required if used / single choice:
- It met my charging needs
- It partly met my charging needs
- It didn’t meet my charging needs
- I’m not sure yet

**4. What, if anything, would you change?**
Optional / short answer.
One specific detail is enough. “Nothing” is a useful answer too.

Do not prime this field with fast charging/capacity examples before collecting feedback; keep those as later coding labels if they arise independently.

### Optional: add a page to the Charj community story

**5. What was happening around you?**
Optional / short answer.
A study session, a long trip, a quiet afternoon—tell us about the moment in a sentence or two. You don’t need an exciting destination or a perfect story.

Avoid “How did Charj save your day?”: it assumes a positive effect. Do not ask friends to stage a testimonial.

**6. Want to share a photo?**
Optional.
One photo is plenty. It can show your Charj, the place, or you—your choice. Please only share photos you took or have permission to share; avoid identifiable people who haven’t agreed to public sharing.

Implementation recommendation: feedback form contains no native file-upload question; invite optional photo by private message to actual Charj account with the same chosen story nickname. Alternatively use a separate optional Google upload form, clearly explain Google sign-in. If a single native-upload form is retained for this friend group, keep photos optional and disclose sign-in friction; do not claim optional upload removes it. No invented handle or unverified photo collection service.

**7. Where should we place this moment on our community map?**
Optional / short answer.
City and country, or just “campus,” is enough. Share a venue name only if you’re comfortable with it being public.

**8. What may Charj share publicly?**
Shown only to story/photo contributors; use two separate yes/no questions:

8a. May Charj share your submitted photo(s) on its own social media and community pages?
- Yes
- No, keep my photos private

8b. May Charj quote your submitted story on its own social media and community pages?
- Yes
- No, keep my story private

No preselected yes. No response means no publication. Research feedback Q1–4 is internal by default and excluded from these permissions. Anyone who shared no story/photo skips permission section. Separate repermission for paid advertising or material supplied to competition/partners; this wording does not authorize all commercial uses or give a legal guarantee.

**9. If we feature your contribution, how would you like to be credited?**
Optional / single choice:
- First name: ___
- Social handle: ___
- No name or tag

Text-entry details via following optional short-answer field; no assumption that Google account display name is publishable credit. Missing choice defaults to no identifying credit. Permission to tag is not permission for ongoing marketing contact.

**10. Want to help shape what’s next?**
Optional / select any:
- Try a future version
- Hear about community updates
- Share another story sometime
- No thanks / not right now

Separate optional contact field: How can we reach you? Add email or social handle only if you selected an invitation above. Use it only for selected purpose. Without contact, no follow-up attempted.

Confirmation:
Thanks for adding your experience to Charj. We’re building this with you—one real moment and one useful suggestion at a time.

Optional community promise: We’d love to share what we learn and what changes next. Only promise a fixed posting schedule once someone can deliver it.

## Short practical variant

If ten items plus conditional fields feels too much, launch Q1–4 as the base, then end with an optional link “Share a photo or a story.” That second link holds Q5–10. This keeps honest feedback open to people without photos or a Google account. Start with this smaller version rather than adding a complex form system.

## Marketing outputs and evidence boundaries

- One real moment + optional city + permitted photo + participant’s own words = a community postcard, e.g. **Charj, out in the world — [city]. [approved quote]**. Do not invent a quote, city, rescue story or customer status.
- Actual recurring feedback → “You told us / We’re testing” content. If device changes are not delivered, do not write “We fixed it.”
- Tag internal dataset by recruitment source (friend, friend-of-friend, independent participant if actually known), free/paid/unknown access, use/carry-only, scenario, outcome, suggestion code, photo/story permission and chosen credit. Do not make participants fill out your whole internal spreadsheet.
- A friend’s free trial is not a paid rental. Friends traveling worldwide is not proof of rental availability, launch coverage, market size or paid adoption in each city.
- With real counts, describe exact sample and denominator, e.g. X of N respondents in an early friend pilot, rather than “students everywhere want…”. Multiple submissions from same person should not inflate unique user counts; do not collect extra identity just to produce a marketing statistic.
- Record permission alongside asset and recheck before posting; do not publish raw responses, account emails, precise movements or unapproved faces.
- Community loop: contributors can hear what changed, choose credit or privacy, and decline publicity without losing the ability to give feedback. Product criticism remains useful participation.
