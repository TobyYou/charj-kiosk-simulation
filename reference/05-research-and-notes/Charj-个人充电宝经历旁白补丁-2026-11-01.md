**TOBY / HOOK**

When was the last time you left home without your phone?

For our generation, our phones are part of how we move through the world. We use them to pay for lunch, open our dorm doors, find our way home, capture memories, and stay close to people across an ocean.

As more of everyday life runs through the device in our hand, access to power becomes part of access to that life.

Then your battery turns red.

Suddenly, you’re checking the percentage, lowering your brightness, and wondering: Will I have enough power to get through the rest of my day?

**SOPHIA:** Hi, I’m Sophia.

**TOBY:** And I’m Toby. Together, we founded Charj.

I actually own a power bank. But it spends a lot of time collecting dust. At night, my phone gets the charging cable. The power bank gets put aside. By morning, I want to take it with me, but I haven’t charged it.

Owning a backup battery doesn’t mean having backup power when I need it.

**SOPHIA:** With Charj, you borrow a charged power bank, keep moving, and return it at another convenient Charj station. The station recharges it for the next person.

We chose power banks with three built-in cables: USB-C, Lightning, and Micro-USB. For compatible devices, there’s no extra cable to remember. Our goal is to make getting power one less thing to worry about.
