TITLE
Take Charj ⚡ How 2 Students Turned a $500 Mini-Grant into a Startup | NEN Pitch

DESCRIPTION
Power up. Take action. Take Charj.

When was the last time you were without your phone? Nine in ten people panic when their battery drops below 20%, and Gen Z starts worrying at about 44%. Charj is our answer: a portable charger rental network. Scan a QR code at a Charj kiosk, grab a power bank, charge on the go, and return it at any Charj location.

In this pitch to NEN and Ronnen Harary, co-founders Toby You and Sophia Peng share how we went from "no idea how to bring it to life" to a working platform, custom hardware, and a community of first users. We incorporated, built the software stack, visited our factory in Shenzhen, and put Charj in people's hands.

How we used the $500 Mini-Grant ($524.74 total):
🔋 20 power banks + USB-C, Lightning, and Micro cables: $230.50
🏷️ 20 custom die-cut Charj stickers: $194.24
🎨 4 artist-edition designs from 3 student artists: $100.00
(the extra $24.74 came from our own pocket)

What we learned:
• 30+ students and faculty tried Charj in just 3 days
• People want a quick top-up, not a full recharge, so we're focusing on fast charging and easy returns
• Charj traveled with users across New York, Massachusetts, DC, Tennessee, and Arizona: lectures, Halloween parties, a pumpkin patch, the golf course, backstage at a theater show, and more
• A power bank is something you wear and show off, so we're making it a fashion accessory with artist-edition designs

What's next: 50 kiosks and 400 chargers across the city, building a network dense enough that a charge is always within reach.

No one should lose their momentum to one dead battery.
