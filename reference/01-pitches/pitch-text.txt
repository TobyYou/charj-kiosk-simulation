[OUR  IDEA  +  BUSINESS  MODEL]  Charj  is  building  a  portable  charger  rental  network:  encounter  a  station,  scan  a  QR  code,  take  a portable power bank, and return it at another convenient Charj location. Our kiosks will be placed in primarily private establishments where people come-and-go: cafes, restaurants, bookstores, etc. These will be done through partnerships with local businesses that will receive a percentage commission per rental from their kiosk location. Not only will partnered establishments receive free installation and monetary commission, they will also gain foot traﬃc from Charj users who walk in looking to scan for a power bank. Our revenue will primarily come from a per hour rental fee. A temporary hold will be put on a customer’s card after successful rental to promote timely returning on power banks; if a customer does not return within a certain timeframe–likely 24 or 48 hours–the deposit will be charged. This rental based power bank business model is tried and true in other parts of the world such as East and Southeast Asia, but is under-developed in North America. Charj hopes to adopt and deliver this model along with our own creative innovations.
