# Charj — Voice-Over Recording Script

Record each clip as its own file (`VO-01-toby.m4a`, `VO-02-both.m4a`, …) so you can re-record one line without redoing the whole thing. Do 3 takes of each clip and keep the best.

**Marks:** `/` = short pause (half a beat) · `//` = full pause (one beat) · **bold** = lean on that word · ↑ = lift (curious, light) · ↓ = land it (confident, down)

Target pace: ~145 words/min. Times are estimates.

---

>
> Owning backup power ↓ doesn't mean **having** it when you need it.

## Recording setup

- **Room:** a closet full of clothes or a small carpeted room. Avoid kitchens, bathrooms and empty rooms (echo). Turn off fans, AC and fridge hum if you can.
- **Mic:** an iPhone works well. Use Voice Memos (Settings → Voice Memos → Audio Quality → Lossless). Hold the phone about a hand's width (6–8 in) from your mouth, slightly off to the side so your breath doesn't pop.
- **Room tone:** before your first clip, record 10 seconds of silence in the same spot. The editor uses it to fill gaps.
- **Same spot, same distance** for every clip, so the voices match across the video.
- **Read the script from a laptop or a second device.** Don't use the recording phone, and avoid paper (it rustles).
- **Warm up:** drink water (no milk or coffee right before), then read VO-01 twice out loud before hitting record.
- **Say the clip number before each take** ("VO-08, take 2"), then pause 2 seconds and start. That makes editing much easier.

## Delivery

- **Smile on the lighter lines.** People can hear it.
- **Talk to one person.** Picture explaining Charj to a friend across the table, not presenting to a room.
- **Slower than feels natural.** Nerves speed you up, and the music and visuals need space.
- **Mess up mid-line?** Pause, then restart from the beginning of that sentence. Don't stop the recording.
- **Before recording VO-15,** confirm the bracketed numbers so you don't have to redo it.
